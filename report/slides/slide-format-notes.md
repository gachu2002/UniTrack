# Slide Format Notes

Use these notes before editing the defense slides.

## Defense Requirements

Yeu cau lam slide bao ve truoc Hoi dong:

- Thoi gian trinh bay: 12-15 phut.
- So trang slide: 18-19 trang.
- Noi dung: tom tat bao cao va qua trinh lam do an tot nghiep. Noi dung chu trong cac cong viec ca nhan da thuc hien va ket qua noi bat.

## Required Structure

### Phan Dau

- Trang 1, trang bia: ten de tai, thong tin sinh vien, ten giang vien huong dan.
- Trang 2, noi dung chinh: liet ke cac noi dung trinh bay.
- Noi dung chinh gom: Muc tieu cua do an; Phan tich bai toan; Thiet ke he thong; Ket qua thu nghiem; Ket luan.
- Trang 3, muc tieu cua do an: dat van de ngan gon va phat bieu muc tieu.

### Phan Than

De tai dinh huong san pham cuoi la ung dung:

- Phan tich bai toan, 1-3 trang: neu cac bai toan can giai quyet trong he thong va giai phap lua chon.
- Noi dung nghien cuu, neu la de tai nghien cuu va ung dung, 1-3 trang: mo ta phuong phap nghien cuu va ket qua.
- Kien truc tong quan cua he thong, 1 trang: hinh ve mo ta cac thanh phan trong he thong, giao thuc neu co va giai thich ngan gon cac thanh phan do.
- Phan tich chuc nang, 1-2 trang: bieu do use-case tong quan.
- Thiet ke CSDL, 1-2 trang: trinh bay bieu do thiet ke.
- Thiet ke hoat dong cho cac chuc nang quan trong, 2-3 trang: trinh bay bieu do hoat dong hoac bieu do tuan tu. Noi dung nay dung bieu do hoat dong thi de trinh bay hon.
- Kien truc phan mem, 1-2 trang: trinh bay bieu do lop.
- Ket qua thu nghiem, 1-3 trang: mo ta ngan gon cach thuc trien khai thu nghiem he thong va liet ke mot so kich ban thu nghiem chinh kem theo man hinh chuc nang.

### Phan Cuoi

- Ket luan, 1 trang: trinh bay tom tat ket luan trong bao cao.
- Trang cuoi, trang cam on: Tran trong cam on cac thay co da lang nghe.

## Presentation Notes

- Khi lam slide, hinh dung voi noi dung tren slide minh se noi nhung gi.
- Sau khi hoan thanh, bam gio va tap noi thu de xem co doan nao truc trac khong.
- Neu can trinh bay cac noi dung thiet ke khac trong khi da het dung luong toi da cua slide thi dua thanh Phu luc.
- Khong copy/paste cac cau van hoac doan van tu bao cao vao slide.
- Khong trinh bay theo chuong hoi, de muc nhu bao cao.
- Hieu tat ca cac hinh ve, cong thuc, ky hieu tren slide.
- Hieu tat ca cac thuat ngu viet tat tren slide.
- Tranh cac tu ngu co sac thai qua manh hoac tuyet doi. Thay vi noi "ABC la giai phap XYZ nhat", nen noi "ABC la mot trong nhung giai phap XYZ nhat".
- Neu co cac chuc nang bo sung, hoac bat cu thay doi lon nao cua he thong so voi bao cao thi phai chi ro trong qua trinh trinh bay.
- Su dung template cua truong.

## Local Style Rules

- Keep the first two original HUST template pages uncounted.
- Count the edited title slide as slide 1.
- Prefer simple, readable slides over dense layouts.
- Use the red template header for slide titles.
- Avoid small subtitle text in the header unless explicitly requested.
- Use large fonts; slides must be readable from a projector.
- Prefer short bullets, compact tables, or existing report diagrams.
- Avoid over-designed cards, decorative boxes, and complex flow graphics unless they clearly help.
- Use report/codebase content as the source of truth.
- Use diagrams from `report/assets/diagrams/` when they explain the section better than text.
- Keep Vietnamese wording natural and presentation-friendly, not copied directly from report paragraphs.
- Keep each slide focused on what will be spoken in 12-15 minutes total.

## Strict Visual Contract

Follow these rules unless the user explicitly asks for a different style.

### Page And Template

- Use the original HUST template PDF as the background.
- Do not edit the original template PDF.
- Keep slide size at `720 x 540 pts`.
- Keep page numbers in the existing bottom-right template position.
- Keep the HUST logo/footer area unobstructed.
- Use the first two template pages as uncounted template pages.
- Count visible defense content from the edited title slide.
- Target 18-19 counted defense slides.

### Safe Area

- Main content should stay inside roughly `x=0.80in` to `x=9.20in` from the page left edge.
- Main content should stay below the header area, roughly `y=-1.30in` or lower from `current page.north west`.
- Main content should stay above the footer area, roughly `y=-6.05in` or higher from `current page.north west`.
- Do not place text over the footer logo or bottom red line.
- Keep at least `0.25in` visual padding around large tables, diagrams, or blocks.

### Fonts

- Use `DejaVu Sans` for normal content slides.
- Use `DejaVu Serif` only for the title slide if it matches the guide/title style.
- Header title font: `24pt` minimum, `24-28pt` preferred.
- Main slide statement font: `20-24pt`.
- Main bullet font: `16-18pt`.
- Agenda item font: `22-24pt`.
- Role/key label font: `16-18pt` bold.
- Table header font: `10.5-12pt` bold.
- Table body font: `10.5-12pt`; never go below `10pt` unless unavoidable for a diagram label already inside an image.
- Footer page number font can remain small because it is not presentation content.
- Avoid header subtitles. If a subtitle is necessary, keep it no smaller than `10pt`, one line only.
- Keep typography consistent across adjacent slides. Do not mix one slide with oversized text and the next with tiny labels unless the tiny labels are inside an imported diagram.

### Text Density

- Prefer 3-5 bullets per slide.
- Each bullet should be one short sentence.
- Avoid paragraphs on slides.
- Avoid copying report sentences directly; rewrite as speaking prompts.
- Avoid absolute or exaggerated wording.
- If a slide needs more than 5 bullets, split it into two slides.
- If a bullet wraps to three lines, shorten it or split the slide.

### Layout Patterns

- Use only one primary layout per slide.
- Preferred layouts: large bullet list, compact table, one diagram with short notes, or one screenshot with short notes.
- Avoid mixing table + diagram + many bullets on one slide.
- Avoid decorative cards and complex flow graphics unless they make the idea easier to understand.
- Keep layouts visually calm: large text, clear spacing, limited colors.
- Do not use tiny explanatory labels around the page.

### Header Style

- Put the slide title in the red template header.
- Header title should be white, bold, and left aligned.
- Do not add a second small line in the red header by default.
- If the title is too long for the header, shorten the title instead of reducing font size.

### Colors

- Primary red: `hustred` / `#C41230`.
- Accent gold: `hustgold` / `#E6A000`.
- Main text: `textdark` / `#1F2937`.
- Muted text: `textmuted` / `#64748B`; use sparingly.
- Do not introduce new colors unless required by an existing diagram or screenshot.
- Use red for headings, labels, bullets, and table headers.
- Use dark text for readable body copy.

### Bullets

- Bullet dot size should be visible from a projector, about `2.5-3pt` radius in TikZ.
- Bullet text should start with a strong verb or noun phrase.
- Keep bullet indentation consistent across slides.
- Do not use nested bullets.
- Do not use punctuation-heavy bullets.

### Tables

- Use tables only when comparing categories or mapping problem to solution.
- Prefer at most 3 columns.
- Prefer at most 4 data rows.
- Use a red table header with white text.
- Keep cell text short; target 1-2 lines per cell.
- If a table needs smaller than `10pt`, split into another slide.
- Use light gray borders, not heavy grid lines.
- Keep row height generous enough for readability.
- Avoid card-like tables where each row is separated by large vertical gaps. Use one contiguous table grid, or replace the table with a diagram if the spacing looks uneven.

### Diagrams

- Prefer existing diagrams from `report/assets/diagrams/`.
- Keep the required design sections diagram-driven: system architecture, use-case analysis, database design, important workflows, and software architecture must include an actual diagram.
- Do not replace a required design diagram with only bullets or a table. If a diagram is too dense, enlarge it, split it across slides, crop to the relevant area, or redraw an equivalent simplified diagram while preserving the design content.
- For database design, show an ERD/data-model diagram or a faithful simplified ERD, not just invariant rules.
- For workflow design, show an activity or sequence diagram, not just a list of steps.
- For software architecture, show a class/struct/component diagram. Because UniTrack uses Go and React rather than traditional OOP classes, a diagram of core structs, interfaces, handlers, and React components is acceptable.
- A diagram should be large enough to read; usually at least `4.8in` tall for portrait diagrams.
- If using a diagram, add at most 3 short bullets or labels beside it.
- Do not shrink a diagram just to fit extra text.
- If the diagram text becomes hard to read, split the diagram across slides or use a simpler diagram.
- Do not edit diagram content inside the slide unless a new source diagram is created separately.

### Screenshots

- Use screenshots from `report/assets/screenshots/` for result/testing slides.
- Use one main screenshot per slide when possible.
- Add only a short caption or 2-3 bullets explaining what the screen proves.
- Do not place more than two screenshots on one slide unless they are very simple and visually related.

### Content Source

- Use `report/chapters/*.tex`, `report/assets/diagrams/`, `report/assets/screenshots/`, and current code/docs as source material.
- Do not invent product scope not present in the report or codebase.
- Keep terms consistent with the report: project, assignment/nhiem vu, submission/bai nop, review/duyet bai, evidence/minh chung.
- When simplifying, preserve technical truth over marketing wording.

### Section Allocation

- Title and opening: 3 counted slides.
- Problem analysis and function analysis: 2-4 counted slides.
- System architecture: 1-2 counted slides.
- Database design: 1-2 counted slides.
- Important workflows: 2-3 counted slides.
- Software architecture: 1-2 counted slides.
- Results/testing: 2-3 counted slides.
- Conclusion and thanks: 2 counted slides.
- If the deck exceeds 19 counted slides, move lower-priority detail to appendix or remove it.

### Verification After Edits

- Rebuild with `latexmk -xelatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error "hust-red-template-copy.tex"` from `report/slides`.
- Check dimensions and page count with `pdfinfo "hust-red-template-copy.pdf"`.
- Check rendering with `gs -dNOPAUSE -dBATCH -sDEVICE=nullpage "hust-red-template-copy.pdf"`.
- Render edited pages with `pdftoppm` and inspect them visually.
- Run `latexmk -c "hust-red-template-copy.tex"` after successful builds.
- Remove leftover `.xdv` files after cleanup if present.
